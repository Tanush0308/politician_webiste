# Client Assets Required

To complete the website to the desired premium standard, the following assets and information must be provided by the client:

## 1. Photography & Video (High Resolution, Original Files)
- **Profile Photographs**: Professional portraits (transparent background or clean studio background).
- **Hero Photographs/Videos**: Cinematic shots of Kailas Dada Patil interacting with the public, giving speeches, or walking in the constituency. A 10-15 second high-quality video for the homepage hero background is highly recommended.
- **Project Photographs**: Before/After photos of development work, road constructions, water projects.
- **Event Photographs**: Public meetings, farmer interactions, youth events.
- **Assembly Photographs**: Photos from Vidhan Bhavan.

## 2. Text & Documentation
- **Official Biography**: A verified, approved Marathi and English biography.
- **Political Timeline**: A chronological list of political milestones with exact years.
- **Project Information**: Verified details of major initiatives (Problem, Action taken, Result, Budget if applicable).
- **Assembly Records**: PDF documents, links, or text of important questions raised in the assembly.
- **News Coverage**: Links to positive, credible news articles or video interviews.

## 3. Brand & Contact Information
- **Approved Slogans**: Official catchphrases (e.g., "जनतेच्या प्रश्नांसाठी, आपल्या मातीसाठी.").
- **Official Social Links**: URLs for authentic Instagram, Facebook, X (Twitter), and YouTube accounts.
- **Contact Details**: Official public phone numbers, email addresses, and physical office addresses for Dharashiv and Kalamb.
- **Constituency Map Data**: If a specific stylized map is desired, boundary data or an approved reference map.

Please provide these files via a secure drive link (Google Drive / Dropbox) organized by the categories above.
