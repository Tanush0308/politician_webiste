## Required Media [CLIENT ASSET REQUIRED]

### 1. Hero
- High-res portrait(s) of Kailas Dada Patil.
- Cinematic landscape/background images (walking with people, addressing a rally).
- Short looping background video (muted, high quality, compressed).

### 2. Initiatives (For each category)
- Farmers: Interacting in fields.
- Water: Near a completed dam/water project.
- Infrastructure: Inspecting road works.
- Health: At a hospital or medical camp.

### 3. Legislative Work
- High-quality photos/videos inside or outside the Vidhan Bhavan.

### 4. Journey/Timeline
- Historical photos from 2017 ZP/PS days.
- 2019 Election campaign/victory photos.
- 2024 Election campaign photos.

### 5. Media & Awards
- Scans or high-res photos of award certificates/trophies.
- News clippings (high-res scans).

## Strategy for missing media
- Use high-quality placeholders (e.g., abstract blurred gradients or neutral constituency landscapes) until client provides official assets. Do NOT use fake/stock politician photos.
